PASSWORD POLICY v2.1

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: IT Security Team

1. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
All corporate account passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters and include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. Passwords must not contain the user's name, username, or any part of their employee ID.

2. PASSWORD EXPIRATION
Passwords expire every 90 days. Users will receive an automated reminder 7 days before expiration. Passwords cannot be reused for 12 cycles.

3. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT
Accounts will lock automatically after 5 consecutive failed login attempts within a 15-minute window. Locked accounts remain locked for 30 minutes, or can be unlocked immediately by contacting the IT Service Desk with identity verification.

4. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
MFA is required for all accounts accessing corporate systems remotely, including VPN, email, and cloud applications. Approved MFA methods are the corporate authenticator app or a hardware security key. SMS-based MFA is not permitted for privileged accounts.

5. PASSWORD MANAGERS
Employees are encouraged to use the corporate-approved password manager for storing credentials. Passwords must never be written down, stored in plaintext files, or shared via email or chat.

6. SHARED AND SERVICE ACCOUNTS
Shared and service account passwords follow the same complexity requirements but expire every 60 days and require documented sign-off from a manager for any access change.

7. VIOLATIONS
Repeated failure to comply with this policy may result in account suspension pending a security review.
